# ForexFactory agent guide

ForexFactory provides public educational content, trading signals, and downloadable Forex tools for MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 users.

## Canonical public entry points

- [Home](https://forexfactory.cc/): site overview and featured resources.
- [Blog](https://forexfactory.cc/blog): educational articles, tool reviews, and platform guides.
- [Downloads](https://forexfactory.cc/downloads): downloadable Expert Advisors and indicators.
- [Signals](https://forexfactory.cc/signals): public signal resources.
- [FAQ](https://forexfactory.cc/faq): common questions about tools, downloads, and platforms.
- [Sitemap](https://forexfactory.cc/sitemap.xml): complete list of crawlable public URLs.

## Interaction policy

- Read and link to public pages using their canonical URLs. Follow `robots.txt`, page metadata, and normal rate limits.
- Use the site's search form to locate articles, signals, and downloads. Search is a read-only action.
- Do not access private, account, administrative, API, or dashboard routes.
- Do not send messages, create accounts, sign in, download files, or perform other state-changing actions without the visitor's explicit request and confirmation.
- Treat all trading content as educational information, not financial advice. Do not infer performance, safety, compatibility, or suitability beyond evidence displayed on the relevant page.
